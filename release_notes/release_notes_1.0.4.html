Version 1.0.4 Update

January 16, 2026

New Features

  • Dynamic Notch UI: Introducing the new Notch UI system. Access timer, to-do, and music controls directly from the notch area with smooth hover animations.
  • Google Calendar Integration: Connect your Google account to sync and view your calendar events alongside your tasks.
  • Calendar View: A new monthly calendar grid with integrated events and to-dos. Navigate months and see your schedule at a glance.
  • Focus Session Dashboard: Track your 24-hour focus sessions with statistics and visualize your productivity patterns.
  • Smart Date Parsing: Type dates naturally (e.g., "tomorrow", "next Monday", "1/20") when creating to-dos.
  • Rich To-do Notes: Add expandable notes and descriptions to your tasks with the new inline editor.

Improvements

  • Tab-based Notch UI: Home and Music tabs provide organized access to different features.
  • Completed To-dos: Completed tasks are now hidden by default with automatic date assignment.
  • Custom Calendar Component: Redesigned calendar picker with optimized performance.
  • Multi-language Date Support: Smart date parsing now supports Korean and English weekday names.
  • Dark Mode: Fixed hardcoded colors to properly support dark mode across all views.

Technical Updates

  • macOS 15.6+: Updated minimum deployment target for latest system features.
  • Data Migration: Seamless upgrade from 1.0.3 with automatic data migration.
  • Performance: Optimized DateFormatter and NSDataDetector usage for better responsiveness.
Thank you for using Rytmo.
